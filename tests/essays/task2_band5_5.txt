Some people say governments should spend more money on public transport, while others think roads for cars are more important. I think both are needed but public transport should get more funding.

First, cars make pollution and traffic jams in cities. If there are good buses and trains, fewer people will drive and the air will be cleaner. Also, not everyone can afford a car, so public transport helps poor people go to work and school.

On the other hand, many towns still need better roads for emergency vehicles, delivery trucks and people who live far from train stations. Completely ignoring roads would cause problems for businesses.

In my opinion, governments should first improve buses, metros and cycling routes in cities, and then maintain roads for necessary traffic. This balance is more useful for the environment and for society.

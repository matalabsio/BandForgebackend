The charts illustrate how energy was generated in one country in 2010 and 2020. The most noticeable change is the decline of coal, which fell from nearly half of total production to about one quarter. Over the same period renewables increased substantially, rising from a minor share to a significant portion of the mix. Oil remained an important source, while nuclear power stayed roughly constant. Overall, the data show a clear shift towards cleaner energy, although fossil fuels still accounted for a large part of supply in 2020.

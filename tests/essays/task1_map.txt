The maps illustrate how a coastal town changed between 1990 and 2020. Overall, the town became more residential and tourist-oriented, with farmland replaced by housing and leisure facilities.

In 1990, farmland occupied the western half of the area, while a small harbour and fishing market stood on the east coast. A single main road ran from north to south through the centre.

By 2020, the farmland had largely disappeared and been replaced by housing estates and a hotel complex near the sea. The harbour was expanded into a marina, and the fishing market was converted into restaurants. A new bypass was built west of the town, reducing traffic through the centre. Green space remained only in a small park south of the hotel.

In summary, the town shifted from agriculture and fishing towards tourism and residential development over the thirty-year period.

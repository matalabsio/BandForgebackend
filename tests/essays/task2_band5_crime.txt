Nowadays crime is increasing in many cities and people have different ideas about how to reduce it. Some think longer prison sentences will stop criminals, but I believe education and jobs are more effective.

Prison can remove dangerous people from society for a while, and maybe it makes others afraid to commit crimes. However, many prisoners return to crime after release because they have no skills or money.

If young people get better education and job opportunities, they may not join gangs. Community programmes and sports clubs also give teenagers something positive to do. Police should still work hard, of course, but prevention is cheaper than locking thousands of people away.

In conclusion, harsh punishment alone does not solve crime. Governments should invest in schools, training and local support so that people have a better choice than criminal activity.
